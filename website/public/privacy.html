Rokuga

Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

Rokuga does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data stored on your Mac

Rokuga stores app preferences on your Mac. Recordings are saved directly to the folder you choose. Rokuga does not upload recordings or preferences to a server.

Permissions

  • Screen Recording access is used to record the screen, a selected area, or a window.
  • Microphone access is used only when microphone recording is enabled.

Captured video and audio remain on your Mac unless you choose to share them.

Analytics and tracking

Rokuga has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the Rokuga GitHub repository.
